# webRTS — Campaign Story Bible

A three-act comedy about a warm rock, a quota, and who deserves to sit on it.
Bright chibi tone. Funny through CHARACTER, not references. Sincerity under the
silly: the goo's grievance is real, and by the end nobody is entirely a villain.

The player experiences all three races in order: **Cogs -> Ooze -> Tempest.**

---

## The arc

**Act I — The Cogs.** You run a scrap depot for the Cogs. Foreman Sprocket wants
the morning quota met; Bearing #7 wants coffee. Reserves keep vanishing
overnight. It is not gremlins. It is goo. You learn the game, meet the "monster,"
and evict it from its nest — a clean, corporate, thoroughly one-sided little war.

**Act II — The Ooze.** Cut to the other side of the slime. You ARE the goo now: a
soft-spoken collective that only ever wanted the warm rock back. You spread,
grow, defend your warren from the Cogs' escalating repossession, and finally
march on the very depot you assaulted in Act I — same map, other side. Sprocket
is now the voice on the far side of the wall, and he is scared. Storm clouds
gather at the end.

**Act III — The Tempest.** Living lightning in floating armor descends and
informs both squabbling neighbors that the warm rock is, in fact, a *battery* —
and it is being repossessed by the sky. (Stub; teased only.)

---

## The cast

**Foreman Sprocket** — pompous middle-management Hub AI. Runs the depot on
quotas, forms, and deflected blame. Says "we are ROBOTS" as if it settles things.
Never his fault. Grows a spine in Act II m3 — when the goo comes for the depot,
the deflection drops and he actually stands and fights, and it's oddly moving.

**Bearing #7** — an over-caffeinated worker who has never seen a threat in its
life and treats every catastrophe as a scheduling problem. Wants coffee. Files
everything. Genuinely delighted by supply caps. The heart's warmth; Sprocket's
cold comedy needs #7's sincerity to land.

**The Ooze (collective voice)** — speaks in lowercase, first-person plural. "we
remember." Politely ominous. Not angry — *homesick*. It narrates Act II. It finds
the Cogs baffling and a little sad ("they count. they always count."). Its
grievance is real: it was scraped off a warm rock it had lived on for a long,
slow time, and it would simply like to lie back down.

**Sprocket-on-the-wall** — same Sprocket, heard from the Ooze's side in Act II
m3. Diegetic radio chatter leaking through the depot wall. The joke of Act I
played back as the horror of Act II.

**Rivet** — Cog quartermaster who only appears via memo. Never speaks aloud; his
QUOTA FORMS (7-B, 7-B-Revised, 12-Q "Unscheduled Biomass") are read by Sprocket
with mounting dread. A running gag and a clock.

**The Herald (Act III)** — the Tempest's voice. Vast, unbothered, speaks in
weather. One line only, for now: *"You have been squatting on our battery. We are
here to collect. Mind the sparks."*

---

## Running gags

- **The coffee break.** #7 wants coffee in m1; is denied; is promised "one
  regulation-sized coffee." The coffee is invoked at every crisis and finally,
  in Act II, is what the goo tastes when it eats the depot ("warm. bitter. we
  understand them a little now.").
- **Quota forms.** Rivet's memos escalate: Form 7-B (Standard Shrinkage) ->
  7-B-Revised -> Form 12-Q, "Unscheduled Biomass." In Act II the goo files
  nothing, ever, and finds the Cogs' paperwork genuinely mysterious.
- **"We are ROBOTS."** Sprocket's all-purpose deflection. In Act II m3 he
  finally says it and it doesn't help.
- **"Unlicensed."** #7 classifies every horror by permit status. The nest is
  "unlicensed." The storm is, presumably, also unlicensed.

## Beat sheets

### Act I — The Cogs

**a1m1 — Rise and Grind** (economy tutorial)
- *Setup:* Morning at the depot. Quota is due. #7 wants coffee; denied.
- *Complication:* The lesson itself — mine, train, build supply, build an
  assembly, hit the scrap target. Sprocket micromanages each step in character.
- *Payoff:* Quota met. #7 gets ONE regulation coffee. The pile has been quietly
  shrinking in the background and nobody's noticed yet.

**a1m2 — Something in the Pipes** (first contact / survival)
- *Setup:* Reserves down AGAIN overnight. Sprocket demands defenses and an
  explanation. The scrap yard is a maze of pipes; goo seeps from a dark corner.
- *Complication:* The scrap pile MOVES. "It has teeth." Four escalating nip waves
  crawl out of the pipes. Mid-mission beat: the first wave triggers a short
  "the pile has teeth" snap-to camera on the seep corner.
- *Payoff:* Waves held. The mystery solved and reframed: the scrap isn't stolen,
  it's being EATEN. Sprocket files it under "unacceptable"; #7 files it under
  "goo, hostile."

**a1m3 — Repo Men** (assault)
- *Setup:* The company ships Thumpers. Sprocket declares an eviction, all
  corporate confidence. The nest sits in a lava-scarred pit; a cliff overlook
  gives the reveal.
- *Complication:* Roll out Thumpers, scout the pit, find the wounded nest — all
  bubbly and "unlicensed." It fights back. #7 is offended by the rudeness.
- *Payoff:* Nest razed, scrap recovered, paperwork filed. "Another perfect day."
  STINGER: from the drains, the goo speaks for the first time. And the sky hums.

### Act II — The Ooze

**a2m1 — The Warm Rock** (creep tutorial)
- *Setup:* You wake as the goo, spread thin and cold, remembering the warm rock.
  Teach: buildings need Goo; spread it from the Nucleus and Vents; units heal on
  it; morph Motes into structures, Nips from the Den.
- *Complication:* A light Cog patrol wanders in, counting. You must reclaim warm
  ground and grow a first Den before they tidy you away.
- *Payoff:* The warren takes root. The goo has a home again, small but warm.

**a2m2 — They Always Count** (defense)
- *Setup:* The Cogs have noticed the warren and filed a form about it. Waves of
  Zappers, then a scripted Thumper siege, come to repossess the ground.
- *Complication:* Escalating waves; mid-mission the Thumper arrives with a
  camera beat and Sprocket's voice leaking through ("Form 12-Q: Unscheduled
  Biomass. Flatten it."). Hold the warren; Barbs and regen and numbers.
- *Payoff:* The siege breaks on the goo's patience. The goo decides, softly, that
  it will not wait to be flattened again. It will go home.

**a2m3 — Homecoming** (assault — Act I m3's map, flipped)
- *Setup:* The depot from Act I, seen from the goo's approach. Same pit, same
  overlook — now yours to climb. Sprocket is the voice behind the wall.
- *Complication:* Assault the Cog depot vs the Cog AI. Sprocket, terrified,
  finally drops the deflection and STANDS — the spine moment. He fights well.
- *Payoff:* The depot falls; the goo lies back down on the warm rock and tastes
  coffee for the first time ("warm. bitter. we understand them a little now").
  STINGER: the sky is not humming anymore. It is speaking. The Herald arrives.

### Act III — The Tempest (stub)

**a3m1 — Weather Warning**
- Teased only. The Herald descends: the warm rock is a battery, and the sky has
  come to collect. Both neighbors, robot and goo, look up at the same sparks.
